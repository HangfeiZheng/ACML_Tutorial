Tutorial Overview

The tutorial is primarily intended for master’s and PhD students, as well as researchers specialising in statistics and data science. Researchers from other disciplines are also welcome, provided they have some familiarity with probability, optimization, statistics and machine learning. No prior knowledge of robust statistics or $\alpha$-divergence is required.

What will participants learn?

This tutorial aims to provide participants with a unified and principled understanding of $\alpha$-divergence as a robust generalization of MLE- and KL-divergence-based learning. It will also equip them with the theoretical and algorithmic tools needed to derive $\alpha$-divergence-induced robust surrogate objectives for their own statistical and machine-learning applications.
